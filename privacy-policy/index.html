Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Woodstock HVAC Pros is a service inquiry website in Headbanger Marketing's portfolio. This page explains how information submitted through this website or its email address is used in the inquiry process.

Information Included in an Inquiry

The form collects the contact details, property location, and project description you enter. An inquiry also includes its originating page and submission time. Email inquiries include the sender details and message you provide. Include only the information needed to review the request.

Routing and Follow-Up

Your inquiry is recorded and routed for review. It may be shared with a relevant service provider so they can respond, confirm coverage and availability, and discuss the proposed work. Submitting a request does not book a visit or confirm a provider's qualifications, pricing, or terms.

Services Used to Handle Information

Website hosting, email delivery, and inquiry processing services handle information as part of delivering these pages and receiving requests. People handling the inquiry and the responding provider may receive the details required for follow-up.

Automated Assistant

Your chat messages are processed by Headbanger Marketing and Anthropic to provide automated answers. If you choose to submit an enquiry and agree to the consent notice, Headbanger Marketing records your contact details, property location and request, and forwards them to the service company identified in the assistant. Coverage, quotes and appointments require separate confirmation. Do not send payment details or other sensitive information through chat.

Search Reports and Browser Settings

These pages do not run a visitor analytics script. We use Google Search Console for search and indexing reports. Hosting and security services may process connection information or use functional browser storage. Your browser settings let you manage cookies and storage.

Questions and Requests

You can contact the email address below about information submitted through this website, including a request to review, correct, or delete it. Identify the inquiry and the change requested. If a responding provider has received your details, you may also need to contact that provider about its records.
