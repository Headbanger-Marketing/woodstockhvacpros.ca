FAQ

Fireplace Installation in Woodstock: Common Questions

How much does fireplace installation cost in Ontario?

Honestly, it varies. Replacing an old insert in an existing opening costs far less than a first-time installation with new gas and venting. A site visit settles it.

How long does a fireplace installation take?

A straightforward electric or direct-vent installation usually takes a day. Opening a wall, running a new gas line, or ordering a specialty unit adds time, and we say so upfront.

Should I repair my old fireplace or replace it?

If the firebox is cracked, the frame is rusted through, or parts have dried up, replacement wins. Worn gaskets and tired fans we fix. We quote both paths honestly.
