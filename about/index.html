Our Story

Rooted Right Here in Oxford County

Woodstock HVAC Pros started on Dundas Street because this is home. We're neighbours serving neighbours across the City of Woodstock and the Oxford County towns that surround it.

Woodstock has always looked after its own. From the century brick homes lining our older streets near the Thames River to the new builds pushing out along the 401 corridor, every household here depends on reliable heat through long, bitter winters and dependable cooling during the sticky stretches that follow. As a family-run HVAC company based right on Dundas Street, we treat every service call like we're walking into the home of someone we'll bump into at the store next week, because most of the time, we will.

We built Woodstock HVAC Pros around one goal: give Oxford County homeowners the same furnace repair, air conditioning service, and honest advice we'd want for our own families. That means showing up when we say we will, explaining what's actually wrong in plain language, and only recommending work that genuinely needs to be done. Over the years that approach has earned us the trust of homeowners from Ingersoll down to Tillsonburg, out to Norwich and Thamesford, and the smaller communities in between like Embro.

Our work covers the full range of residential comfort, furnace and air conditioner repair and installation, high-efficiency heat pumps sized for our real Ontario climate, ductless mini-splits for older Woodstock homes that were never built with ductwork, fireplace installation, smart thermostat upgrades, and thorough duct cleaning that clears out the dust our older housing stock loves to collect.

Our Commitment

How We Treat Your Home

We know letting a tradesperson through your door isn't always convenient, so we work to make it easy. Every technician on our team is licensed, insured, and trained to respect your space, with drop cloths down, clear communication throughout, and a clean-up that leaves things the way we found them. You'll get a straightforward explanation of the problem, an upfront price before we start, and a repair done right the first time.

Behind every service call is a deeper promise: stand behind the work, use quality parts, and never push a full replacement when an honest repair will do the job. That's the standard we've built our name on across Oxford County, and it's the reason families in Woodstock call us back season after season.
