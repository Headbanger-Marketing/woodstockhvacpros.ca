About the Website

Woodstock HVAC Pros

A service request point for properties in Woodstock and the listed surrounding communities.

Purpose

Start With the Property and the Work

Use this website to describe the property, equipment, and work you want reviewed. The service guides help you prepare the enquiry and questions for a proposal.

Coverage, contractor availability, qualifications for the proposed work, pricing, and terms should be established as part of the request and proposal process.

How Your Request Is Reviewed

The enquiry team receives the enquiry and shares it with Jayden's Mechanical for review. The team uses your contact details to ask about the property and proposed work.

The request starts a discussion. Before work is agreed, ask the provider to confirm coverage, the qualifications required for the task, the assessment scope, pricing and appointment availability. A form submission does not book a visit or request an emergency response.

The equipment guidance helps you prepare questions; it is not a diagnosis or a property inspection. Each service guide links to relevant public technical guidance. Check the instructions for your actual equipment with a qualified contractor.

What to include

  • Exact equipment models and design assumptions
  • Installation work, exclusions and property constraints
  • Gross price and separately confirmed rebates
  • Assessment, schedule and completion terms

Also include the property address, your contact details, and who can approve the work.
